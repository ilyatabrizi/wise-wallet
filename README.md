# Wise Wallet

A personal euro wallet simulator with one virtual card. Open the published GitHub Pages site in iPhone Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App and tap Add. Launch from the new icon once while online to cache the app.

In Settings you can change the sample balance, cardholder, display digits and monthly limit. Add or edit income, spending, investments, assets, liabilities and goals. Net worth is cash plus investments and assets minus liabilities.

The optional six-digit PIN encrypts the wallet on this device. Keep your PIN safe. Clearing the app's website data can erase your entries. Your edits stay on your device.
